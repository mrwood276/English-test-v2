- **Fix**: announcements are now once per signed-out period — `sessionExpiredAnnounced` flag in `core/api.js`, reset by a `staff:signed-in` event dispatched in `app.js#showApp()`. Users still land on sign-in with the first explanation; redundant re-renders are gone.
